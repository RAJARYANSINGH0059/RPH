# Model Card: PS05-X Account Triage Model

**Model:** `random_forest` (scikit-learn RandomForestClassifier, 300 trees, max depth 8, balanced class weights)
**Artefact:** `ml/artifacts/triage_model.joblib` · SHA-256 `db1ce406622a3d4d86df312a0336258b772026e43ff8504f40aed96917485e8e`
**Feature schema:** `ml/artifacts/feature_schema.json` · **Metrics:** `ml/evaluation/metrics.json`
**Dataset version:** 1 · **Generator version:** 0.3.0 · **Scenario version:** 1
**Training command:** `python ml/training/train.py --seeds 12 --size small` (96 s on a laptop CPU)

## Intended use

A **triage cue** that ranks accounts in a case for analyst attention, shown next to the transparent rules baseline. It is one input to a human review. It is displayed as "triage score" with the explicit label *requires human review; not a probability of guilt*.

## Prohibited use

- Any automatic enforcement action (freeze, lien, complaint filing).
- Labelling a person or account as "criminal", "mule" or "fraudster" from the score.
- Use on operational data without re-evaluation on authorised, labelled real cases.
- Treating the score as a calibrated probability.

## Training data (synthetic only)

All data were generated by the PS05-X Synthetic Case Factory and passed through the real ingestion pipeline. **No real police, bank or personal data were used.** The label means *"this account was generated as a mule-pattern participant (first-layer, second-layer or exit account) in the scenario"*, not criminality.

| Split | Rows (accounts) | Positives | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|
| train | 1,463 | 416 | 140 |
| validation | 420 | 119 | 40 |
| test | 1,094 | 321 | 108 |

Splits are **by scenario** (seed × template × difficulty), never by row. The `qr_marketplace` typology is **entirely held out** in the test split. Seeds 1000–1011; 6 scenario families × 4 difficulty levels per seed.

## Features (16, from `app.graph.engine.account_features`)

in_degree, out_degree, tx_in, tx_out, total_in_minor, total_out_minor, out_in_ratio, median_holding_hours, unique_counterparties, velocity_tx_per_day, amount_concentration, fan_in, fan_out, missing_field_ratio, duplicate_count, time_uncertain_ratio. No names, phone numbers, VPAs or other identifiers are used as features.

### Calibration

Reliability bins (test): scores in 0.0–0.2 → observed rate 0.8 %; 0.4–0.6 → 28 %; 0.8–1.0 → 90 %. The mid-range is **over-confident** (score ~0.45 vs observed 0.28). The score is therefore shown as a ranking cue, not a probability. Full bins are in `metrics.json`.

## Known limitations and failure modes

- Synthetic-only training: feature distributions follow the generator's assumptions (fan-in, short holding, splitting). Real mule behaviour differs; performance will not transfer.
- Accounts with no statement of their own (second-layer / exit accounts) are scored only from counterparties' statements, so their features are partial.
- The model cannot see infrastructure evidence; it is a financial-features model only.
- Threshold 0.5 was not tuned for an operational cost trade-off.

## Reproducibility and integrity

- `python ml/training/train.py` regenerates the dataset deterministically and retrains.
- The API loads the artefact only if its SHA-256 matches `triage_model.sha256`; `tests/e2e/test_model_inference.py` verifies loading, scoring and checksum refusal.
- If a future retrain does not beat the baseline, the script deletes the artefact and records `why_not_retained` in `metrics.json`; the UI then shows the rules baseline only.
